About

Raiden — 雷電, "thunder and lightning." The name is the sound: rolling low-end you feel in your chest, hats that crackle like static, and drops that land like a strike.

Raiden plays house — deep and hypnotic early, tech and peak-time late. Long blends, patient builds, no gimmicks. The kind of set where you look up and two hours are gone.

Behind the decks it's a Pioneer DJM-V10 and two CDJs — six channels of room to layer, cut, and sculpt in real time. That's why the booth on this site is playable: it's the actual instrument, scaled down to your browser.
